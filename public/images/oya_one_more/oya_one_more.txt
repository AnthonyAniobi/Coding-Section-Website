Oya One More is a fast, thrilling press-your-luck dice and card game that dares you to chase points—or lose them all in a single roll! Each turn is a tension-filled race to match your dice to the symbols on a card and climb toward victory. Play it safe, or push for “one more” roll to claim massive points before your luck runs out. Gameplay Overview * Draw a card and roll five dice. * Match dice to the pattern shown on your card to score points. * Bank your score safely—or keep rolling to draw another card for even more rewards. * Bust once, and everything you earned that turn is gone! The more dice you match, the higher your score climbs. Complete all dice on a card and collect a sweet +30 Bonus Points! The thrill comes from deciding whether to stop or go for one more—a choice that keeps every round unpredictable and exciting. Special Cards and Power Plays Among the 90 total cards, 20 are Special Cards that bend the rules and keep everyone guessing. Play them at just the right moment to change your fate: * Steal 20 Points from the leader and close the gap. * Change a Die to any value you need. * Re-roll unused dice for a comeback chance. * Walk away with double points before it’s too late. Use them wisely—once you bust or finish your dice streak, they’re gone! Why You’ll Love It * Quick to learn, endlessly replayable. * Every decision feels risky and rewarding. * Great mix of luck, strategy, and daring. * Compact and portable—perfect for game nights, parties, or travel. Whether you’re a cautious scorer or a fearless risk-taker, Oya One More will keep your heart racing with every roll. Match smart, push your luck, and when you think you’ve done enough—just whisper those famous words: “Oya One More!”
